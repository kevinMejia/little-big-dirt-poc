# Audio auditions — awaiting approval

Open **Asset Showroom → Audio Auditions**, or `/viewer/audio.html` on the local server.
This delivery implements the requested research and playable shortlist stage.
No candidate audio is imported, fetched or played by the driving game.
No engine, tree, rock, cannon, Turbo Boost or Word Hunt audio hooks were changed.
Physics, gameplay, settings, models and world generation are unchanged.

## How to review

- Use Listen on a card, then Play sample to repeat it. Loops audition for 12 seconds.
- The two engine candidates have separate preview volumes, an enable switch,
  a throttle/speed pitch control and an eight-second idle/acceleration/cruise/deceleration demo.
- At the bottom of the scrollable library, audition the combined cannon, tree,
  Turbo Boost and Word Hunt timing sketches. These use illustrative timing and
  are not synchronized to the game yet.
- Keep for review marks choices in this page only. Export shortlist downloads
  JSON with `approved:false` and `integrated:false`. It does not apply anything.
- Reply in chat with candidate IDs or your exported shortlist and explicit approval.
  Integration, category settings and actual gameplay synchronization follow approval.

All 23 candidate recordings are offered as CC0 by the linked source pages.
Each card exposes its source and license. The complete manifest records download
URLs, original download names, original/processed SHA-256 hashes and processing.
Kenney pack license files are preserved under `assets/audio-candidates/licenses/`.

The audition files total about 389 KiB. They are AAC-LC in M4A containers, mono
44.1 kHz at 96 kb/s, with level matching and transient headroom. Loop excerpts have
150 ms cyclic crossfades. Loop smoothness, sound character and musical suitability
still need your listening approval. Freesound recordings use official high-quality
MP3 previews; their lossless original downloads require Freesound login. These
are genuine downloaded preview recordings, not generated or invented downloads.

## Engine provenance

Dirty's candidate is ReadeOnly's prepared slow diesel idle loop, not a confirmed
backhoe recording. Stinky's is a different recording by kyles of a rattling old
school-bus diesel, proposed for its heavy truck character. Neither is presented
as an authentic recording of these particular vehicle models. Approval can reject
or replace either before any engine audio enters Play.

## Motocross Madness 2 reference

The reference is sudden cannon attack, a long comic airborne interval, a falling
whistle, then a landing payoff. The [cannon entry in Soundeffects Wiki](https://soundeffects.fandom.com/wiki/Hollywoodedge%2C_Civil_War_Cannon_Shot_PE091601)
identifies a Hollywood Edge commercial-library sound in both games; a [separate
whistle entry](https://soundeffects.fandom.com/wiki/Sound_Ideas%2C_CARTOON%2C_WHISTLE_-_FALL_WHISTLE%2C_LONG)
identifies a Sound Ideas falling whistle in Motocross Madness 2. These are fan
catalog identifications, not verified publisher cue sheets. Player accounts also
recall the [cannon and cartoon falling-whistle contrast](https://www.reddit.com/r/gaming/comments/rhwzmj/still_my_favorite_world_boundary_mechanic/).
The timing sketch is an interpretation of those references and your brief, not
an exact reconstruction measured from original game audio.

No reuse permission for those commercial recordings was established. No original
game audio was downloaded, embedded or sampled. The cannon audition uses Kenney's
CC0 low-frequency explosion, Luke.RUSTLTD's CC0 wind and a soft impact. The crunchy launch alternative and digital descent tone were removed at your request. The demo now plays boom → wind → landing.

## Existing audio and later integration

Read-only inspection found oscillator/noise effects in `index.html` for tree/rock
impacts, horn and landings, plus a separate gesture-unlocked `HuntAudio` context in
`WordHuntAudio.js`. This phase leaves them intact. The eventual shared manager
should reuse/consolidate that Web Audio approach after approval, cache decoded
buffers, route Master/Engine/Effects/Music buses, persist volume/mute preferences
through the existing Settings system, and stop background loops on navigation.

The current tree and rock impact callbacks already enforce contact cooldowns.
A later adapter can observe existing fall/landing and flight state transitions
for one-shot sounds without changing thresholds or physics. Cannon timing must
follow the actual launch/flight/landing lifecycle, not the fixed sketch timing.
Engine pitch should ramp on a persistent loop source rather than restarting it.
These gameplay integrations and persistent category controls are deliberately
pending your approval, not partially enabled in the game.

## Rebuilding auditions

`tools/build_audio_candidates.py <audio-research-cache> <ffmpeg.exe>` prepares the
selected recordings, not whole sound packs. Original pack downloads and Freesound
HQ previews are kept in an external authoring cache; download URLs are in the
manifest. FFmpeg is authoring-only; no runtime dependencies were introduced.
