# Approved audio library

Open **Asset Showroom → Audio Auditions** to listen to the 23 approved CC0 clips and inspect each source URL and license. The two rejected alternatives have been removed. Foliage is now a 0.45-second burst.

The sounds are integrated in gameplay. Manage each sound’s volume and enable switch under **Settings → Audio**. See [Game audio controls](GAME_AUDIO.md) for defaults, categories, saving and testing limitations.

The audition page’s shortlist export remains a review-only selection and does not change gameplay settings. Full source provenance and processing details are in `assets/audio-candidates/manifest.json`.
